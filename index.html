RC Broz Junk Removal

We don’t just remove junk. We clear the way for your peace of mind. Whether it’s household clutter, construction debris, or estate cleanouts, our team handles it all with efficiency and a commitment to making your space usable again. Fast, affordable, and hassle free.

Appliances

We safely and efficiently remove unwanted appliances of all sizes, including refrigerators, washers, dryers, ovens, and more. Whether upgrading, remodeling, or clearing out space, our team handles the heavy lifting and ensures proper disposal or recycling—so you don’t have to lift a finger.

Furniture

Old or unwanted furniture can quickly take up valuable space. We remove bulky items like sofas, mattresses, tables, and dressers with care and efficiency. From single pieces to full room cleanouts, we help you reclaim your space while handling proper donation, recycling, or disposal.

Garage Clean-ups

Garages have a way of becoming catch-all storage spaces over time. We clear out clutter, old boxes, tools, and unwanted items to help restore order and free up usable space. Whether it’s a light clean-up or a full garage overhaul, we handle the sorting, hauling, and disposal so you can enjoy a clean, functional garage again.

Landcare

We provide reliable land care services focused on clearing, cleanup, and maintaining outdoor spaces. Our team helps keep your property clean, safe, and well-maintained.
